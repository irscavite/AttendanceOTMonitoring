ATTENDANCE & OT MONITORING - STRAIGHT DUTY BREAK CORRECTED

Upload all web files in this folder together, replacing the existing webapp files.
This package builds on the prior IT Record Maintenance update; keep the included
Firebase Realtime Database rules if they have not yet been published.

On a Regular Day, the first 8 paid hours of the assigned shift are regular.
Worked hours beyond the shift count as OT. A separate nine-hour Straight Duty
schedule includes one unpaid one-hour break and therefore contributes 8 paid
OT hours. Example: assigned shift 06:00-15:00 (8 regular paid hours), normal
OT 15:00-18:00 (3 OT hours), Straight Duty 18:00-03:00 (9 clock hours minus
1 break hour = 8 OT hours): total OT is 11 hours. Additional Straight Duty OT
In/Out fields are optional; enter them only when hours go beyond that block.
Breaks are excluded from the employee worked-hours preview. The earlier
06:00-15:00 Straight Duty schedule plus 15:00-20:00 now counts 5 OT hours
because the 06:00-15:00 block includes one break hour.
Normal (non-Straight Duty) OT minimum and rounding rules are unchanged.
Already approved records retain their saved OT totals.

Supervisor accounts outside the Admin department now have an OT / Leave Summary
navigation page and dashboard shortcut:
- Select a From/To date and optionally an employee. The default is the current
  payroll cutoff. Dates are inclusive.
- Department total: approved OT hours by OT duty date; pending hours are shown
  separately. OT amendments already included in the original approved total
  are never added twice.
- View OT totals by employee and individual OT records with approval status.
- View leave history by employee. A multi-day request appears when any of its
  leave dates overlap the chosen range.

Admin department Supervisors and HR accounts keep their existing HR reports.
No data is deleted or changed by this reporting page.
