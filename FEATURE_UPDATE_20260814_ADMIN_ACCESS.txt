ADMIN / HR ACCESS UPDATE

1. Removed "HR" from IT -> Account Access role choices.
2. HR staff are treated as employees of the Admin Department.
3. For Admin Department employees, IT can grant these additional modules independently:
   - Employee Master List
   - Leave & Approved OT
   - Cutoff
   - Department Summary
4. Admin employees keep their normal Employee self-service pages (Dashboard, Attendance & OT, My OT Submissions, File Leave, My Leave Requests).
5. Existing legacy managed accounts with role "HR" are shown as Employee and treated as having all four Admin modules when edited, allowing migration without losing access.
6. Module permissions are saved in Firebase account profiles as moduleAccess.
