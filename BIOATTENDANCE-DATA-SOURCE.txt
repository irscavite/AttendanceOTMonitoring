STRICT FIREBASE SOURCE SPLIT

ATTENDANCE / ROSTER FIREBASE (AUTHORITATIVE)
https://bioattendance-7cc7a-default-rtdb.asia-southeast1.firebasedatabase.app/
- employee_master = Attendance roster (employee must stay visible even with no scan)
- attendance scans = Time In / Time Out
- No scan on selected date = No Scan (employee remains in table)
- Full Name / Employee No. / Department / Position come from BioAttendance employee_master
- OTMonitoring employee master must NOT add/remove Attendance roster members

LEAVE FIREBASE
https://otmonitoring-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com/
- Approved leave comes from hrLeaveRequestsV1
- Attendance page overlays fully Approved leave by Employee No. + covered date
- Shows Approved Leave + Leave Type + Reason
- Leave data is read through the authenticated OTMonitoring Firebase SDK connection

TIME RULES
- First valid scan = Time In
- 2nd/latest valid scan = Time Out
- Worked hours deduct 1 hour lunch when Time Out exists
- Night shift is grouped by duty date

This build intentionally ignores old Present attendance stored under hrDailyAttendanceV1 for the live Attendance page.
