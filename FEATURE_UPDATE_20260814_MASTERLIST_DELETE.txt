EMPLOYEE MASTER LIST DELETE ACTION

- Added Delete button beside Edit in Employee Master List.
- Delete requires a confirmation modal.
- Delete removes the employee from the active Employee Master List and schedule/setup configuration.
- Employee profile overrides, shift/schedule/location/day-off settings, SL/VL overrides, pending IT account request, and supervisor roster notification are removed.
- Historical attendance, overtime, and leave records are retained for reporting/history.
- If a Firebase Authentication/system login was already created, the master-list delete does not remove that Authentication user. IT must manage/disable that login separately.
