EMPLOYEE MASTER LIST UPDATE — 2026-08-14

Visible columns are now:
- Employee Name
- Position
- Leave
- Absent
- SL
- VL
- Action

Changes:
- Employee No. is no longer displayed in the Employee Master List.
- Department and employment status are no longer shown as table columns.
- Edit is now only for SL and VL remaining credits.
- SL/VL credit adjustments are saved to Firebase-synced app data.
- Approved With Pay SL/VL continues deducting from the adjusted balance.
- Leave and Absent counters remain clickable to view attendance history.
- Employee Master List Excel export now follows the simplified visible columns (without Action).
